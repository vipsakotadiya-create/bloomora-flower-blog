FEATURED ARTICLE

The Timeless Language of Roses

Few flowers carry as much cultural meaning as the rose. Its shape, fragrance and remarkable range of colors have made it a recurring symbol in gardens, literature and art.

Roses belong to the genus Rosa, a diverse group of flowering plants with hundreds of species and thousands of cultivated varieties.

More than a beautiful bloom

Roses have been grown by people for centuries. Their beauty, fragrance and diversity have made them an important part of gardens and cultural traditions.

“A rose does not need to explain why it is beautiful.”

What different colors represent

Red roses are commonly associated with love. White roses may represent purity or remembrance, while pink roses often communicate appreciation and affection.
